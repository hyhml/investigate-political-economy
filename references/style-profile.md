## Status

## Stable traits observed

- Begin from a concrete policy announcement, its money, institutions, and declared purpose.
- Let suspicion arise from timing, scale, participating firms, and surrounding geopolitical events.
- Move outward through corporate actions before making the broadest systemic judgment.
- Treat firms as trails of behavior across time, not static profiles.
- Interleave factual narration with the author's reactions and political judgments.
- Widen from individual transactions to alliances, resource regions, war, finance, and global production.
- End by extending the argument historically rather than merely summarizing sections.

## Hidden organization

Use a backstage movement resembling:

official account → anomaly → mechanism → corporate traces → recurring direction → international structure → political judgment → historical widening

Do not expose this movement as a long numbered report. Use only the headings that arise naturally from material, often a broad overview, selected enterprise movements, and a synthetic conclusion.

Do not announce the entire conclusion at the beginning. Establish enough factual ground for the reader to experience the structure becoming visible.

## Sentence and paragraph movement

- Allow uneven paragraph and section lengths.
- Use chronological sequences inside company sections.
- Permit recursions: return to an earlier suspicion after new evidence changes its meaning.
- Use transitions that register thought in motion, such as noticing, reconsidering, or widening the frame.
- Prefer long synthetic paragraphs when connecting several material processes, balanced by short declarative pivots.
- Preserve some roughness and authorial pressure; do not polish every paragraph into symmetrical institutional prose.

## Political voice

- Permit explicit terms such as imperialism, resource extraction, regional fortification, war preparation, and capital conduits.
- Let the concrete flow of credit, ownership, commodities, labor, and coercion give these terms meaning instead of pausing for textbook definitions.
- Treat economic and military power as connected without assuming every action follows a single secret command.
- Analyze asymmetry between U.S. military-financial power and Chinese industrial-supply-chain power.
- Keep the author's political judgment visible. Counterevidence should sharpen its scope, not erase it through ritual balance.

## Avoid

- Think-tank report symmetry.
- Repeated `not X but Y` formulas.
- Visible evidence tags in article prose.
- Identical company case templates.
- Tables that replace narrative development.
- A conclusion fully stated before the investigation begins.
- Generic academic definitions that interrupt the argument.
- Invented typos, copied grammatical errors, or unsupported causal leaps.
- Copying distinctive sentences from the sample.

## Updating this profile

When the user supplies a new sample:

1. Extract recurring and sample-specific traits separately.
2. Compare them with this profile.
3. Present meaningful conflicts to the user.
4. Update stable traits only after user confirmation.
5. Preserve project-specific variation rather than averaging all samples into generic prose.
